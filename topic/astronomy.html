Astronomy & Planetary Science

Astronomy investigates the origin, evolution, physics, and chemistry of celestial objects outside Earth atmosphere. High school and pre-college students explore stellar nucleosynthesis, black holes, galactic structures, and planetary geology while utilizing real light-curve photometry data from missions like Kepler and TESS.

Essential Astronomy & Planetary Science Formulas & Laws

Stefan-Boltzmann Luminosity Law
L = 4π · R² · σ · T⁴

Total power radiated by a star as a blackbody in terms of stellar radius and temperature.

Exoplanet Transit Depth
ΔF / F = (R_planet / R_star)²

Fractional drop in stellar flux during planetary transit reveals exoplanet radius.

Wien's Displacement Law
λ_peak = b / T (where b ≈ 2.898 × 10⁻³ m·K)

Peak emission wavelength of blackbody radiation inversely relates to surface temperature.
